01 — The practice Gurugram · Est. 2018

Good design is a series of decisions made in the right order.

AarchiZone is an architecture, interiors and engineering practice based in Gurugram. We work on homes, workplaces and retail spaces — usually from the first site visit to the last site instruction.

The studio was founded in 2018 by principal architect Dheeraj Bansal, after more than a decade of practice across architecture and project consultancy. That range is the point: fewer handovers, fewer gaps, one team accountable for the whole.

03 — What we do One team · Three disciplines

Three disciplines. One accountable team.

Architecture, interiors and the engineering between them. We can lead a project end to end, or join at the stage where you need us.

04 — How we work Brief → handover

Drawn, detailed, delivered.

A clear sequence, with decisions made at the right time. You always know what stage we are in and what comes next.

01

Listen & survey

We start on site. Sun, access, neighbours, structure, budget, the way you actually live or work. The brief gets sharper before a line is drawn.

02

Draw & test

Options, not one fixed idea. We test massing, planning and light, then develop the scheme with you until the plan feels inevitable.
