Before you call

A few useful answers.

No preparation is required. If you have a site address, a rough brief or a drawing, bring it — but a conversation is enough to start.

Do I need drawings before I call?

No. A site address, a rough idea of what you want to build or change, and a sense of your budget range is enough for a first conversation. If you have drawings, send them along.

Can I visit the studio?

Yes. Call or email first so we can make sure someone is available and you have the right amount of time. The studio is in Civil Lines, Gurugram.

Do you work outside Gurugram?

Most of our work is in Gurugram and the NCR. For the right project we can travel further; the first conversation will tell us whether that makes sense.

What happens after I send the form?

The form opens your email app with the details filled in. Once you send it, we will read it and reply with a few questions or a time to talk. Nothing is stored on this website.
