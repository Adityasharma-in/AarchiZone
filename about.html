Architecture is a long conversation.

AarchiZone Design Consultancy was founded in Gurugram in 2018. We are an architecture, interiors and engineering practice — small enough that the person who draws the line is still around when it is built.

Our work is mostly in and around Gurugram: homes, independent floors, commercial blocks and retail. The scale changes; the method doesn't. Understand the site, agree the brief, test the plan, detail it properly, and stay close through construction.

We are not interested in a signature look. We are interested in the right decision for the place, the budget and the people who will use it — and in making sure that decision survives contact with the site.

Principal Architect

Design, then make it buildable.

Dheeraj leads the studio. He graduated from Chandigarh College of Architecture in 2005 and has spent his career across architectural design, project architecture and technical consultancy — including time with Lines Architects, Creative Group, Eigen Technical Services and STUP Consultants.

He founded AarchiZone in 2018 to bring design, engineering and delivery closer together. His approach is practical: resolve the big moves early, then spend the time on the junctions that make a building feel considered.

That means one person is accountable for the whole drawing set — and for the questions that come up when the drawing meets the site.

How we think

Four things we do not compromise.

These are not slogans. They are the checks we run on every drawing, every sample and every site decision.

01

The site comes first

Orientation, access, light and neighbours are not constraints to solve later. They are the beginning of the plan.

02

Draw it properly

A beautiful render is not a building. We invest in working drawings, details and coordination before work starts.

03

One team

Architecture, interiors and engineering sit at the same table. Fewer handovers, fewer gaps, faster decisions.

04

Stay to the end

We are on site for samples, snags and handover. The last five percent is usually what people remember.
