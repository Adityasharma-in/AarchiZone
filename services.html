The sequence

What you get at each stage.

A typical project, simplified. The exact deliverables are tailored to the scope and the approvals your site needs.

01 Brief & feasibility

Site study, brief document, area statement and a clear view of what the plot can hold.

02 Concept

Plans, elevations, 3D views and a material direction — enough to test the idea before committing to it.

03 Technical design

Working drawings, structural and MEP coordination, finishes, details and a BOQ your contractor can price.

04 Delivery

Site visits, samples, coordination, snag lists and a final handover. The drawing is checked against the building.

Questions

Asked early, answered honestly.

If your question is not here, call the studio. A short conversation is usually faster than an email thread.

Where do you work?

Mostly in Gurugram and the NCR. For the right project we can travel further; the first conversation will tell us whether that makes sense for the scope and the site.

Can you take on interiors without the architecture?

Yes. We can join an existing shell or a building designed by someone else, provided the drawings and site conditions are clear. We will review what exists before confirming scope.

Can you work with our contractor?

Yes. We can design, detail and coordinate, and visit site at agreed stages. We are also comfortable working alongside a contractor you already trust — the important thing is a clear drawing set and a single point of coordination.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scale, approvals and site conditions. A residential interior may take a few months; a new building can take a year or more. We set a stage-wise programme before starting so you know what to expect.

How do fees work?

Fees are based on scope, scale and the level of site involvement. We put the scope and fee in writing before work begins, so there are no surprises. We do not publish a rate card because no two projects are the same.

Do you handle approvals?

We prepare and coordinate the drawings and can liaise with your consultant or contractor through the approval process. The exact scope is agreed at the start, along with any third-party fees.
